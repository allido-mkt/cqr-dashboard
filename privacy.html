Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026

Overview

CQR Dashboard is an internal reporting/dashboard system for Channel Quality Report views and related operational reporting.

This page is provided for Google OAuth app branding review and public reference.

Access

CQR Dashboard is intended for authorized users. Public visitors may see the dashboard entry page, but dashboard access is restricted by sign-in and access checks.

Google Sign-In

When Google Sign-In is used, the dashboard may receive basic Google account identity information needed to identify the signed-in user and check access, such as email address, name, and profile image.

The dashboard does not ask users to enter a Google password. Google account authentication is handled by Google.

Dashboard Data

The dashboard displays internal reporting information for authorized use. It is not intended as a public data source.

Do not submit sensitive personal information through public pages on this site.

Contact

For questions about access to CQR Dashboard, contact the system owner or administrator who provided access to this dashboard.
